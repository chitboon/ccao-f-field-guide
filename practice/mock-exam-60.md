## Before you start

- Set a **120-minute** timer. One sitting. No lookups, no notes, no Claude.
- Target pace: **2:00 per item.** At the 60-minute mark you should be at or past item 30.
- Domains are **not** labelled — deliberately. The real exam does not tell you which domain an item is drawn from, and knowing it cues the answer.
- **Multi-response items state how many to select.** Partial selections score **zero**. There is no partial credit.
- Record answers on one line per item (e.g. `NN. B` or `NN. B,D`). Do not mark the paper as you go.
- The answer key is a **separate file**. Do not open it until the timer stops.

Flag-and-return is encouraged: if an item costs more than ~2.5 minutes, put down your best guess, mark it, move on. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so **never leave an item blank**.

> Two cues have been deliberately engineered out of this paper: the correct answer is **not** systematically the longest option, and two items key a model-tier decision as correct, so "change the model" is not automatically wrong. Do not rely on either heuristic here or in the real exam.
